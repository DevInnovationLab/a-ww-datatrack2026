CODEBOOK - HOUSEHOLD WATER QUESTIONNAIRE (clean data)
=====================================================
Missing values are empty cells (in the survey: -999 = Don't know,
-888 = Declined to answer, both already set to missing).


data/households.csv - one row per submission (1,293 rows)
---------------------------------------------------------
  key             SurveyCTO submission ID (unique row ID)
  hh_id           A2. Household ID
  village_id      A3. Village
  survey_date     Date of interview (YYYY-MM-DD)
  consent         B1. Consent to interview: 1 = Yes, 0 = No
                  (if No, the interview ends: everything below is missing)
  hh_size         C5. People living in household
  hh_children     C6. Children under 5 in household
  hh_watersource  C7. Main drinking water source: 1 = Piped, 2 = Protected
                  well, 3 = River or stream, 4 = Trucked, -666 = Other
  stored_yn       D1. Drinking water stored now: 1 = Yes, 0 = No
  storage_time    D6. Hours since water was collected (asked if D1 = Yes)
                  Answer options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 18, 24, 36, 48, 60
                  hours, and 99 = More than 72 hours
  treat_chlorine  E1. Days chlorine was added to drinking water, past 7 days
  treat_boil      E2. Days drinking water was boiled, past 7 days


data/children.csv - one row per child under 5 (1,587 rows)
-----------------------------------------------------------
  key             SurveyCTO submission ID of the child's household interview
  child_no        Child number in the roster (section G)
  hh_id           A2. Household ID
  village_id      A3. Village
  child_age       G2. Child's age (months)
  diarrhea_2d     G3. Diarrhoea in the past 48 hours: 1 = Yes, 0 = No
  diarrhea_7d     G4. Diarrhoea in the past 7 days: 1 = Yes, 0 = No
                  (asked only if G3 = No)
